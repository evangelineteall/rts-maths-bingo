Maths Bingo · Always, Sometimes, Never · Countdown Targets

How this works

A target number and a set of numbers appear on the board. Students use +, −, ×, ÷ to try to reach the target — they don't have to use every number, and each number can only be used once. Give them a minute or two, then press Reveal to show one worked route to the target.
