How this works

Each student draws their own grid on a whiteboard and fills it with any numbers they like from 0 to 100. Click a year group below to see its topics, pick one to review, then press Start game. The site will call out a maths question at a time — students work it out and cross off the matching number if it's on their board. A live 0–100 grid on the caller screen shows every number called so far, so you can check students' boards at a glance.
